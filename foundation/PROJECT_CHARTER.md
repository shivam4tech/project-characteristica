# Project Characteristica — Charter

**Working Title:** Project Characteristica — An AI-Assisted Reconstruction and Extension of Leibniz's Characteristica Universalis

**Date Initiated:** 2026-08-16

**Status:** Phase 1 — Literature Review & Historical Reconstruction

---

## Mission Statement

We are undertaking a serious interdisciplinary research programme inspired by Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz's unfinished projects of the **characteristica universalis**, **ars combinatoria**, **calculus ratiocinator**, **lingua universalis**, and **scientia generalis**.

The ultimate objective is to determine whether a genuinely novel, formally defensible, computationally implementable successor to Leibniz's project can be constructed using contemporary logic, linguistics, ontology engineering, knowledge representation, formal semantics, automated reasoning, and artificial intelligence.

The project must be conducted as if intended for serious publication in philosophy of language, philosophy of logic, history/philosophy of science, formal philosophy, logic, or an adjacent interdisciplinary journal.

## Central Research Hypothesis (§XXXII)

> Previous universal-language projects attempted to place the burden of semantic understanding inside the language itself. Modern AI potentially allows us to move part of that burden into an intelligent interpreter while retaining a formally constrained representation beneath it.

**Must also investigate the opposite hypothesis:**

> AI does not solve the fundamental problem because semantic interpretation by neural models introduces a new form of ambiguity and epistemic opacity that undermines the Leibnizian ideal of unambiguous representation.

The project must be able to conclude either way.

---

## Radical Possibility (§XXXIII)

Investigate whether the true Leibnizian object is **a universal method for transforming concepts into formally manipulable structures** — where universality belongs to the **operations**, not the dictionary.
