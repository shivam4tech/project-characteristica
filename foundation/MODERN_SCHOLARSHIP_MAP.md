## Part A: The Logic-Reconstruction Seam

The single most important scholarly seam: systematic reconstruction of Leibniz's logical fragments as a working logic. This is where "what Leibniz actually had" gets decided.

### A.1 The Lenzen School (systematic reconstruction)
- ✅ **Wolfgang Lenzen** — central figure of the reconstruction school. Via IEP "Leibniz: Logic" (https://iep.utm.edu/leib-log/, inspected 2026-08-16): Lenzen's reconstruction finds **four calculi** in Leibniz's fragments:
  1. **L1** — algebra of concepts (deductively equivalent to Boolean algebra of sets)
  2. **L2** — quantificational system ("indefinite concepts" function as quantifiers ranging over concepts)
  3. **Strict implication calculus** — obtained from the strict analogy between concept-containment and proposition-implication
  4. **Plus-Minus-Calculus** — theory of "real addition/subtraction"; set-theoretic containment without elementhood or complement
- Key interpretive claims from the same source:
  - Leibniz's drafts contain **inconsistencies** from treating real subtraction by analogy with arithmetic (Lenzen 1989 genesis study); Dürr (1930) gave a conservative reconstruction restricting subtraction
  - Couturat judged the intensional approach "necessarily bound to fail"; Kneale & Kneale (1962): Leibniz "never succeeded in producing a calculus which covered even the whole theory of the syllogism"
  - Rehabilitation line: Dürr (1930), Rescher (1954), Kauppi (1960), Poser (1969), Ishiguro (1972), Rescher (1979), Burkhardt (1980), Schupp (1982), Mugnai (1992)
  - Critics of intensional logic: Liske (1994), Swoyer (1995), Schupp (2000) — argue it gives rise to inconsistencies/paradoxes

## Part D: The 2026 AI Attempt — VERIFIED

- ✅ **EXISTS.** Slavenskoj, Danslav (Charles University in Prague). "On the Realization of Leibniz's Characteristica Universalis." Written Feb 22, 2026; posted SSRN Apr 15, 2026. **SSRN abstract id 6291378**; DOI 10.2139/ssrn.6291378. 5 pages. Keywords: characteristica universalis, formal notation, universal language, artificial intelligence. Paper statistics at time of inspection: 17 downloads / 63 abstract views.
- Abstract (as displayed on SSRN, inspected 2026-08-16):
  > "In 1666, Leibniz proposed the characteristica universalis: a notation expressing any human knowledge unambiguously, compositionally, and language-neutrally. Every subsequent attempt failed for the same reason: no reader could simultaneously handle formal structure at arbitrary complexity and understand natural language content across all human languages. We identify this as the necessary and sufficient condition for a solution. The emergence of capable AI systems (~2024) satisfies this condition. We present Lingenic: a host notation embedding extensible formal systems, from propositional logic to dependent type theory, with natural language content carried unmodified at native semantic grain. Lingenic satisfies Leibniz's requirements. The calculus ratiocinator is out of scope: Lingenic addresses notation only; the reader supplies reasoning. This document is written in Lingenic. A notation discharges its claim by demonstrating validity within its own formalism."
- Preliminary extraction from abstract alone (full analysis pending PDF retrieval):
  - **Definition of problem:** notation-level only — unambiguous, compositional, language-neutral expression of any knowledge.
  - **Necessary & sufficient condition claim:** a reader able to handle arbitrary formal structure AND understand natural-language content across all languages = AI (~2024).
  - **Architecture:** host notation embedding extensible formal systems (propositional logic → dependent type theory); NL content embedded "unmodified at native semantic grain."
  - **Explicit scope exclusion:** calculus ratiocinator OUT OF SCOPE — reasoning supplied by the reader (the AI/human).
  - **Self-referential validation:** the paper itself is written in Lingenic.
- **Immediate critical observations (to be developed into LINGENIC_CRITICAL_ANALYSIS.md):**
  1. By excluding the calculus ratiocinator, Lingenic abandons exactly the half of Leibniz's programme that makes verification possible — "the reader supplies reasoning" reintroduces precisely the epistemic opacity Leibniz wanted to eliminate.
  2. "Natural language content carried unmodified" concedes that semantic analysis is NOT performed — meaning resides in opaque neural interpretation, not in the notation.
  3. Therefore Lingenic arguably does NOT satisfy compositionality or unambiguity in Leibniz's sense; it satisfies them only for the formal skeleton, not the content.
  4. **What remains fundamentally unsolved if Lingenic is correct:** everything on the semantics/reasoning side — the two-layer division of labor, ambiguity representation, provenance, uncertainty, the AI-as-fallible-interpreter problem. This is where Project Characteristica's novelty space lies.
  5. The AAAI-25 recursive-isomorphism result further shows notation choice cannot carry deep novelty — reinforcing point 4.
