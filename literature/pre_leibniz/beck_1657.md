# Cave Beck — The Universal Character (1657)

**Gate ruling: PASS (narrow), medium confidence** — passes only as design-counterexample: G8 (machine-relevant principle: indexical coding alone adds zero semantics) and G6-adjacent decodability lesson. ## Relevance justification

Beck's scheme isolates the single variable CE-01 cares most about — codebook lookup versus compositional generation — by having *only* the former: every word replaced by a decimal numeral indexing a Latin–English lexicon, with the advertised payoff "Latin taught in a few hours." Its historiographic grouping with Dalgarno/Wilkins as the third major English universal-character scheme makes it the period's own control condition. A counterexample that cleanly demonstrates why numbering-without-order fails is machine-relevant design knowledge (it prefigures both token-ID codebooks and their overhead trap).
